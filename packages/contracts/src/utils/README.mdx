# Utilities

Libraries and helpers used by the account and its modules.

- {Multisig}: Encoding and decoding of multisignatures and their signer sets.
- {CrossChainSignatureChecker}: Verification of ERC-7964 crosschain signatures.
- {ERC8043Utils}: Encoding helpers for the ERC-8043 init mode.
- {MultisigCoordinator}: Coordinates FHE inputs and decryption rights between the signers of a multisig account.
